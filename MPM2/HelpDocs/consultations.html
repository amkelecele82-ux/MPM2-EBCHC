Clinical Consultations & Folders

The Patient Folder view is designed for clinical staff to record vitals, notes, and prescriptions during a visit.

Recording Vitals

Under the "Vitals & notes" tab, enter the patient's readings for today. The system will automatically flag abnormal readings:

  • If a Blood Pressure reading is too high (e.g., 165/105), it will turn red to alert the attending doctor.
  • BMI is calculated automatically when you input Height and Weight.

Clinical Notes & Prescriptions

Use the text boxes to document the patient's presenting complaint and your final diagnosis. From here you can:

  • Set a Follow-up date or create a referral to another department.
  • Click "Add prescription" to assign medication. You will need to specify the medication name, dosage, and frequency.
Always click "Save & close consultation" when the visit is over. This clears the patient from the "Waiting" queue on the main dashboard.
