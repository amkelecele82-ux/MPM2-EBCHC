Patient Management

This module allows you to register new patients and search the clinic's database for existing records.

Registering a New Patient

Click "Add new" from the Patient Records screen to open the registration form. You must navigate through the tabs to complete the file:

  • Personal Details: Enter the ID number, full names, Date of Birth, and contact details. Note: Fields marked with a red asterisk (*) are mandatory.
  • Medical History & Chronic Conditions: Record known allergies and ongoing conditions.
  • Use the "Next" button to move between tabs, and click "Register & save" when finished.

Searching for a Patient

The Patient Records screen displays a searchable grid.

  • Use the search bar to find a patient by their Folder Number or ID Number.
  • Use the dropdown filters to sort by "All patients" or specific statuses.
  • Select a patient from the list and click "Edit" to update their info, or "View folder" to proceed to clinical notes.
