Welcome to the East Boom CHC System

This digital clinic management system is designed to improve service efficiency, reduce patient waiting times, and securely manage electronic health records.

Located

East Boom Community Health Centre (CHC) is an urban CHC under Umgungundlovu Health District situated at uMsunduzi Municipality, in Pietermaritzburg the capital of KwaZulu-Natal Province.

East Boom CHC is located in the lower end of Pietermaritzburg central business district, close to the N3 national highway.

The CHC is divided by a busy road (Boom Street). A bus and taxi parking area is situated on the corner, below Boom street health centre, resulting in easy access to the Health Centre for communities from the outlying areas of PMB.

Getting Started

Use the navigation menu on the left to find specific instructions for the module you are working in.

  • Dashboard: For tracking daily stats and the waiting room queue.
  • Patient Management: For registering new arrivals and searching for existing records.
  • Consultations: For clinical staff to record vitals, notes, and prescriptions.
Need urgent help? If the system is frozen or displaying database errors, please contact the IT Administrator immediately.
