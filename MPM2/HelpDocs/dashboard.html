Using the Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a live overview of clinic operations for the day.

Daily Statistics

At the top of the screen, you will see daily counters for:

  • Patients today: How many have been seen vs. how many are still waiting.
  • Appointments: Total bookings, including unconfirmed ones.
  • Urgent cases: Patients who need immediate attention.

Today's Queue (Waiting Room)

This section shows a live list of patients currently in the clinic. Pay attention to the status badges:

  • Urgent: Patient needs immediate attention (e.g., high BP).
  • Waiting: Patient is in the queue for a general or follow-up visit.
  • Seen: Consultation is complete.

Alerts & Reminders

Keep an eye on this panel for system notifications, such as patients overdue for lab results or pharmacy stock replenishment reminders. You can clear these by clicking "Dismiss all".
